Our story

We're a group of college students who got tired of note-taking apps built for taking notes, not for passing exams. Our notes looked great, and we still walked into tests unsure of what we actually knew. So we built the tool we wanted:

  • Organized around the exam, with a countdown and a daily goal
  • Every slide tied to the learning objective it covers
  • Your own questions, written right next to the slide
  • Progress you can see, lecture by lecture
  • No AI shortcuts: building the pack is the studying
