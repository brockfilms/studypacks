Study Packs Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Study Packs does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

Your content stays yours. The PDFs you import and the notes, ink, questions and progress you create are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they also sync through your own private iCloud account, which only you can access. The developer never receives this data.

Device link. The optional Mirror slides feature connects your own nearby devices directly over your local network or Bluetooth. Nothing passes through the internet.

No AI services. Study Packs does not send your content to any AI or third-party service.

Children. Study Packs collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact. Questions about this policy: open an issue on our support page.
